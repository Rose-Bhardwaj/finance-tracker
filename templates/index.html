Upload Transaction Screenshots

Supported: SMS / notification screenshots from your bank or apps like Swiggy, D-Mart, etc. The app will use AI OCR to extract the amount and description.

Tip: To simulate uploading a folder, select multiple PNG/JPEG images at once in the file picker.

What Happens Next?

  • Your screenshots are sent to an AI Vision model to extract transaction text and amounts.
  • Each transaction is automatically categorised (Rent, Groceries, Swiggy, DMart, etc.).
  • The dashboard shows category-wise charts, current vs last month comparison, and alerts.
  • The AI assistant page lets you chat about your spending and set future goals.
